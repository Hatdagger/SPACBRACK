SPACBRACK — art direction samples

Rough samples to pick a direction before real art. Everything is drawn at the Neo Geo's 320×224 and scaled up. Pick a letter per section, or mix them.

1 · Main character (seen from behind)

The hero is a big foreground sprite with their back to the camera, so the back view matters most. No space suits.

2 · Scenery / stages (over-the-shoulder view)

A wide background strip scrolls as you turn left/right (shown auto-panning). The hero stays still in front. Things you can use get a bouncing cue. Letterbox bars top and bottom carry the location name and button hints.

Full Launch Complex strip (640 px wide, two screens). The frame shows what one screen sees while turning.

3 · NPCs (one crew style per location)

Front-facing, the same scale as they'd stand in the scene. Bosses get a fancier version of their location's outfit.

4 · Map (static picture + moving cursor)

One still image. The cursor jumps between the 6 locations with the D-pad (shown cycling here). A label shows the selected location.

Samples only: palette, proportions and layout are what's being judged, not the final pixel art.
