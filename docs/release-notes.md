**SPACBRACK v0.12 — playable demo** (one entry on the *New Games Ideas* demo disc)

A retro-futurism RPG where every fight is a falling-brick versus match.

### How to play
1. Download **SPACBRACK-v0.12.zip** below and unzip it.
2. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Firefox (Windows, Linux or Android). Nothing to install.
3. Tap or press a key once for sound. Plug in a controller and press a button on it to connect it. Double-click for fullscreen.

### In this demo
- Adventure: six station locations, each with its own house rule and stage perk (Launch Countdown, Holograms, Fold Garbage, Interest, Save States, Pull the Lever). Crew fights to 20 lines, bosses to 40.
- Collect monster cards that level up; defense moves unlock first. Each boss drops a unique card that carries its location's perk (used with the card button).
- Chrono Station: face a terminal running your old loadout, then Past You.
- Win all six tokens for the ending.
- Versus: vs CPU (8 levels) or local 2P, with every rule and card.
- Placeholder chiptune music and sound effects, placeholder art.

Controls are listed in README.md. This is a prototype: art, audio and balance are all placeholders.
